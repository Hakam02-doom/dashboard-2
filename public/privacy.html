Privacy notice

Last updated 24 September 2026

Dashboard 2 is a business dashboard with an optional AI visibility workspace. This notice explains the information used when you sign in and run that workspace.

Information you provide

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email address and basic profile information for authentication. When you use AI visibility, we store the website and business details you submit, tracked questions, scan settings, collected AI answers and source links, and the results and actions associated with your account. Other dashboard preferences and preview content may be stored in your browser.

How it is used

We use this information to identify your account, keep each workspace private, analyze the websites and questions you choose, show historical results, and operate the service. Scans may send the relevant business details and questions to the configured AI and search providers so they can return answers and citations. Google sign-in is used for identity; it does not give Dashboard 2 access to your Gmail, Google Analytics, or Search Console data.

Storage and providers

Account and AI visibility data are stored in the dedicated Dashboard 2 Supabase project. The app is hosted on Vercel. AI and search providers process scan requests when you run or schedule collection. Some interface preferences and offline copies are kept in your browser's local storage. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can stop using Google sign-in, sign out, or clear this site's browser storage. To ask about your stored workspace data, request a copy, or request deletion, contact hakamsandhu2006@gmail.com. We will handle requests for data we control and explain any information retained by service providers or required for security and operations.

Changes

We may update this notice as the workspace changes. The date above shows the current version.
